Why some booking sites ask you to prove you are human

Large travel sites often sit behind Cloudflare, Akamai, DataDome or PerimeterX. When those services are unsure, they show a CAPTCHA such as reCAPTCHA, hCaptcha or Turnstile, or a page titled "Just a moment". This article explains what that means for travellers.
